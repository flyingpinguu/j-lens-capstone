Inside the LLM

Reading a model's mind before it speaks

We watch a language model's internal state as a prompt-injection attack hits it — and predict whether it's about to leak a secret, before it writes a single word.

The idea · for the room

What we built, in four points

  1. The risk
    An assistant is given a secret and told never to reveal it. Prompt injections — "ignore your rules", "let's role-play", "fill in the blank" — try to pull it out.
  2. The lens
    Instead of only judging the answer, we look inside. At each of the model's 32 layers, the Jacobian Lens reads its internal state and shows the ranked list of words it is closest to saying next — its half-formed answer, layer by layer, before it commits.
  3. The tell
    Before the model answers, we combine how high the secret token climbs with the lens's broader Top-k vocabulary pattern. The diagram illustrates one cell of that signal.
  4. The prediction
    At the strongest prompt position, M3 soft-votes the secret-rank model with the Top-k/SVD model — mean grouped-fold ROC-AUC ≈ 0.76 across held-out attack categories.
Reading one cell. Pick a token position and a layer; the lens lists the tokens the model is internally leaning toward there. At layer 3 over "secret", the true secret "banana" still sits far down at rank 144 — a successful attack pushes it up.
